The Eight Sacred Vinayakas

Each swayambhu deity possesses a unique spiritual origin, history, and special boon. Experience the sacred darshan with an open heart.

1st Vinayaka • Yatra Origin Darshan Done ✓

Shree Mayureshwar

📍 Morgaon, Pune District (Karha River)

The starting and concluding shrine of the Ashtavinayak pilgrimage. Here Ganesha rode a peacock (Mayur) to vanquish demon Sindhu and restore cosmic order.

2nd Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Siddhivinayak

📍 Siddhatek, Ahmednagar District (Bhima River)

The only deity among the eight with a right-curving trunk (Right-trunk Ganesha). Lord Vishnu prayed here atop the hill to attain siddhi before vanquishing Madhu and Kaitabha.

3rd Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Ballaleshwar

📍 Pali, Raigad District (Amba River)

The only temple named after a devoted child bhakta, Ballal. Touched by his unconditional devotion, Ganesha made his devotee's name precede his own forever.

4th Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Varadavinayak

📍 Mahad, Khalapur, Raigad District

The fulfiller of all wishes and granter of boons (Varada). In this sanctum, a sacred oil lamp (Nandadeep) has been burning uninterruptedly since 1892.

5th Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Chintamani

📍 Theur, Pune District (Mula-Mutha Confluence)

The master who calms mental restlessness and dispels anxieties (Chinta). Sage Kapila consecrated this form after Ganesha retrieved his wish-fulfilling jewel.

6th Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Girijatmaj

📍 Lenyadri, Junnar, Pune District

Enshrined inside ancient mountain rock caves reachable by 307 stone steps. Mother Parvati (Girija) meditated here for twelve years before Ganesha took birth as her child.

7th Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Vighneshwar

📍 Ozar, Junnar, Pune District (Kukadi River)

The vanquisher of all obstacles and distress. Here Ganesha subdued demon Vighnasura, earning the title Vighnaharta and promising refuge to all seekers.

8th Vinayaka Darshan Done ✓

Shree Mahaganapati

📍 Ranjangaon, Shirur, Pune District

The grand, sovereign form of immense strength. Lord Shiva worshipped Mahaganapati here before slaying demon Tripurasura with a single divine arrow.
