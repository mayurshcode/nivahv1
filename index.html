अष्टविनायक Eight Temples

॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ अष्टविनायक यात्रा

Eight Sacred Forms of Lord Ganesha

The Ashtavinayak Yatra is a revered pilgrimage to eight swayambhu (self-manifested) Ganesha temples in Maharashtra. Each murti has its own unique form, legend, posture, and blessing. The sacred pilgrimage traditionally begins and ends at Morgaon.

Traditional order: Morgaon → Siddhatek → Pali → Mahad → Theur → Lenyadri → Ozar → Ranjangaon → return to Morgaon.

The Eight Sacred Vinayakas

01 • MAYURESHWAR

श्री मयूरेश्वर

Morgaon, Pune district

First and last stop of the yatra. Ganesha here rides a peacock and is believed to have slain the demon Sindhu. The black-stone shrine is guarded by a Nandi at the gate.

02 • SIDDHIVINAYAK

श्री सिद्धिविनायक

Siddhatek, Ahilyanagar district

The only Ashtavinayak murti with the trunk turned right (Dakshinavarti). Set on a hillock beside the Bhima River, this shrine is believed to grant siddhi — spiritual accomplishment.

03 • BALLALESHWAR

श्री बल्लाळेश्वर

Pali, Raigad district

The only Ganesha named after a devotee — the boy Ballal. The east-facing stone temple has eight carved pillars for the eight directions, and the sacred prasad here is besan ladoo.

04 • VARADAVINAYAK

श्री वरदविनायक

Mahad, Raigad district

Giver of boons and success. The weathered swayambhu idol was found in a nearby lake. An eternal oil lamp (Akhand Nandadeep) here is said to have burned without pause since 1892.

05 • CHINTAMANI

श्री चिंतामणी

Theur, Pune district

Named for the wish-fulfilling Chintamani jewel restored to sage Kapila. The nearest Ashtavinayak from Pune, located at the sacred confluence of the Mula, Mutha, and Bhima rivers.

06 • GIRIJATMAJ

श्री गिरिजात्मज

Lenyadri, Pune district

Parvati’s son, carved directly into Cave 8 of the Lenyadri rock-cut hills. Reached by stone steps, the sanctum is illuminated only by natural daylight without electric lamps.

07 • VIGHNESHWAR

श्री विघ्नेश्वर

Ozar, Pune district

Remover of obstacles. After vanquishing the demon Vighnasura, Ganesha took this name. The shrine stands on the Kukadi River behind high stone ramparts with a golden pinnacle.

08 • MAHAGANAPATI

श्री महागणपती

Ranjangaon, Pune district

The most powerful form, worshipped by Shiva before the battle with Tripurasura. The east-facing Peshwa-era temple is aligned so that the morning sun’s rays fall upon the deity.
